Build the Blender project in this folder ($folder, your working directory) so that it turns the script at $input into a finished short video.
Read the script first, then plan the shots, one script each, and list them in render.json in the order they play.
Swarmify renders the project at quality `$quality` when you finish: that must be one of render.json's presets.
Use what the video needs of the stage's other inputs and the world's assets, when below names any, by their paths.
